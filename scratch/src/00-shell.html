    The twelve wings

    How it is built

    One HTML file, no build step, no tracking. Three.js draws all 88 plaques, frames, plinths, the twelve hall backdrops and the star field above them in about fifty instanced draw calls, and the renderer sleeps whenever the room is still — the plan view below mirrors the exact geometry the GPU sees. Each wing is its own hall: a lit backdrop in its own colour, a name plate on the dado, partitions on either side and a constellation running through its plaques.

    The plan view is clickable: pick a wing to send the camera there. Favourites live in this browser only (localStorage), nothing leaves the page.
